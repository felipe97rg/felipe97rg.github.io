3rd place · UNDP Colombia Data Hackathon · 2026 · Individual

Disaster Displacement Risk in Colombia

For each of Colombia's 1,102 municipalities and every month, the model estimates the probability of a disaster event affecting people, how many could be displaced, and an 80% uncertainty range (low and high scenario) to plan shelters and aid. Built for UNDP Colombia's hackathon, where it won 3rd place.

Leak-free municipal history (prior years only, smoothed toward department and national rates), public cartography (location, population, 5 nearest neighbors) and CHIRPS satellite rainfall, the biggest gain (+3.6 validation points; a placebo added nothing). Four LightGBM models chosen from 63 experiments with a decision rule fixed in advance; live AUC rose from 0.759 to 0.780.

MSc AI · Uniandes · 2026 · Team project

Early Prediction of Child Stunting

With Fundación Canguro: predicts whether a baby will develop chronic malnutrition during the first 24 months using only the maternal profile and birth data, so limited follow-up resources can be prioritized. Evaluated with repeated stratified cross-validation and confidence intervals; the served model reaches ROC-AUC 0.79 at 12 months.

DVC data pipeline, MLflow tracking on EC2, model packaged as a wheel, FastAPI service (7 endpoints, 55 tests) and a Streamlit dashboard, deployed with Docker Compose on AWS EC2.
